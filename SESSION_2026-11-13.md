## Decisions

- **Prices stay.** Undercutting Higgsfield Plus would cap the batch at about $900, below the target.
- **Don't re-extend the Sunday deadline.** EU consumer law treats renewed countdowns as false urgency; Italy fined a shop about €2M in June 2026.
  - After Sunday, use the real scarcity: the batch prices end when it sells out.
  - For the next push, use a new real occasion: a Black Friday bonus on 27 Nov.

## What happened

The owner topped up $10 of Anthropic credit, and it ran out within two days.

**The five `job_analysis_failed` events on 6–7 Oct were all
`Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API`.** The balance
had already run out once. After the top-up on 7 Oct, the $10 paid for
4 analyses and 3 fix jobs:

| Job | Shots | Pairs | Fixes | Rough Claude cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clayfire | 48 | 60 (cap) + 40 standalone | 8 | ~half of the $10 |
| farveh | 9 | 36 | 8 | ~a third |
| billy | 6 | 15 | 1 | the rest, with ordinaryfox |
| ordinaryfox | 2 | 1 | 0 | |

Every one of them was a free user. **The main cost is the after-fix check:**
`run_verify` re-runs `compare_pair` over every pair of the output video. The
output has new keyframe URLs, so the Firestore cache never hits. A fix job
therefore pays for a second full scan on top of the original one.

### Fix quality (real users, 29 attempts)

- 17 verified (59%), 8 not confirmed (refunded), and 4 failed: 3 blocked by
  Runway content moderation, 1 by our clip-window bug.
- By job: 4 of 10 users had everything fixed, 2 some, and 4 nothing.
- **Only 2 of the 10 downloaded the result.** clayfire had 8 of 8 verified and
  never downloaded. Free output was 480p with a watermark, which may be why.

### Unit economics

1 credit = 1 second, sold at $0.42–0.56. Runway is prepaid (about 226k
credits, roughly $2,260), so it costs no cash until that balance runs out. With
Runway at $0, a paid fix job roughly breaks even on Claude:

- Revenue counts **seconds**, but the Claude cost counts **shots and pairs**.
- AI shots are 1–5 seconds long, so a job sells only 10–30 credits.

Decision: **quality first.** The scan and the full re-scan stay on Opus as
they are. No cheaper model, and no partial re-scan. Instead, charge for
fixing and raise the price a little.

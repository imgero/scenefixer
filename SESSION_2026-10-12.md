## Shipped in this PR

- **Free scans run on Claude Haiku 5.5 with a hard $0.20 cap per scan**
  (`FREE_SCAN_MODEL`, `FREE_SCAN_BUDGET_USD` in `modal_app/detect.py`).
  - A `SpendMeter` prices every call from `response.usage`. Once the scan has
    spent its budget, it skips the rest of the Claude calls (pairs, standalone,
    holistic, bbox validation).
  - Worst case measured with stubbed usage: 60 pairs + 40 standalone =
    100 calls, **$0.175**.
- **Paid scans are unchanged:** Opus 5 for pairs, Opus 4.8 for the rest. Paid
  means a paid plan, or any purchased credits (`tier_for_job`).
- **Anything unconfirmed scans free:** no owner, a missing user document, or a
  failed read.
- **Verify uses the same tier as the job's scan** (`tier_for_verify`, via
  `scanTier` on the job). An Opus verify of a Haiku-scanned job would report
  everything Haiku missed as damage the fix did.
- **Every scan logs its real cost:**
  - on the job: `scanCostUsd` and `scanBudgetHit`;
  - in PostHog `job_analysis_completed`: `claude_cost_usd`, `claude_calls`,
    `budget_hit`, `calls_skipped`, `scan_tier` and `scan_model`.
- **Daily scans:** 1 a day without credits, 3 a day while holding credits
  (`lib/types.ts`).
- **Pricing copy** no longer promises "every error found" on a free scan.
  Credit holders get "our full deep scan".
- **Modal now deploys from GitHub:** `.github/workflows/deploy-modal.yml` runs
  `modal deploy modal_app/app.py` on every push to `main` that touches
  `modal_app/`, or by hand from the Actions tab. It needs the
  `MODAL_TOKEN_ID` / `MODAL_TOKEN_SECRET` repository secrets.

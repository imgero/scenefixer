## 1. Shot detection replaced with TransNetV2: SHIPPED (accd165, modal deploy)

This is the third real user in a row whose result was ruined by shot
segmentation, not by the detection model:
- 09-23: letterbox bars hid 9 cuts.
- 09-25: a dark cut scored 25.6 against the 27.0 threshold.
- 09-29: dissolves and a fade.

ContentDetector compares each frame to the previous one against one
threshold, so a dissolve (the change spread over ~15 frames) never crosses it.

**Measured, not assumed.** I took all 86 unique real uploads (md5-deduped from
191 jobs), normalized them with the production crop and transcode, and scored
every frame with both detectors. I then hand-labelled every point where either
detector reacted at all, from 9 frames spanning -1 s to +1 s:

| Label | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---|
| C | hard cut | 303 |
| D | dissolve or fade | 35 |
| N | not a cut | 229 |
| ? | ambiguous, excluded | 29 |

The 257 points where all three signals agreed were taken as real after a
40-point random sample came back 40/40.

| Detector | Hard cuts | Dissolves | False cuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| ContentDetector 27.0 (was live) | 257 | 4 | 30 |
| ContentDetector 20 | 292 | 12 | 108 |
| TransNetV2 0.5 | 286 | 17 | 0 |
| **TransNetV2 0.3 (shipped, production code path)** | **287** | **24** | **7** |
| TransNetV2 0.1 | 299 | 27 | 31 |

A false cut is the expensive error: it can become a repairable false finding
that a user pays for. That is why the threshold was chosen on false cuts
first, and why lowering the pixel threshold was a dead end.

What shipped:
- **`decompose.detect_shots` uses TransNetV2 at 0.3.**
  - Frames are decoded at 48x27 and timed by packet PTS. Variable-frame-rate
    uploads had drifted up to 0.73 s under the old index/average-fps clock.
  - Inference runs in chunks, and the minimum shot length stays at 0.5 s.
  - The model load restores Python's and numpy's RNG state, because the
    package seeds them globally.
- **`softCutsMs`.** Weak 0.1–0.3 peaks are stored per shot. `run_detect`
  drops a within-shot scene-change finding when its shot has one: Opus and
  the detector agreeing means a missed cut. Without a peak the finding stays,
  unrepairable, as before.
- **`isBlank`.** A shot is blank when every keyframe's 99th-percentile luma
  is ≤ 32. Measured on 431 shots, black tops out at 20 and the darkest real
  footage starts at 51. Blank shots are never compared.
- **Modal image.** CPU-only torch 2.14.0 and `transnetv2-pytorch==1.0.5` were
  added, and scenedetect was removed.
- **`PROMPT_VERSION` v18**, because re-analysis reuses keyframe URL strings.
- **`scripts/shot-eval/`.** Labels, a fetch script, `run.py` (exits 1 on a
  regression), and `test_shots.py` (synthetic, no model needed).

**Verified in a Modal container on the Qatar video.** It found 6 shots.
- The fade to black is its own shot, so it gets marked blank.
- The 19.5 s dissolve is a soft peak inside the 13.3–22.8 s shot.
- The day-to-night empty room is correctly kept as one shot.

**Open: speed.** Detection took 40 s for this 43 s video in the container,
about 4x slower than on a Mac, because Modal's default CPU reservation is
small. A 300 s video could add 4–5 minutes. Setting `cpu=` on
`run_process_job` would fix it, but it costs money on every job, so it waits
for an owner decision.

## 2. Retried uploads reuse their job: SHIPPED (171f490, vercel --prod)

The first read, "every upload creates two jobs", was wrong. Firestore
`createdAt` shows these are retries:
- 38 of 232 jobs were stuck in `uploading`.
- In 31 of those, the same owner created another job within 30 minutes.
- Only 5 left files in Storage (284 MB).

The harm is to users, not to storage costs:
- My Jobs lists the stuck jobs as "Uploading" forever.
- A beta tester's single slot is claimed when the job is created, so retrying
  after a dropped connection was refused with "already used".

What changed:
- **`DropZone` keeps the pending job** and uploads a retry of the same file
  (same name, size, lastModified, type and hint) into it while its signed URL
  has more than 10 minutes left.
- **`lib/uploads.ts` `isAbandonedUpload`** treats a job still `uploading`
  after the 1 h URL TTL as abandoned.
  - My Jobs hides it.
  - The job page says "This upload didn't finish… nothing was charged", with
    a link to upload again.
  - This covers all 38 old stuck jobs with no cleanup job.
- **New `job_create_failed` event**, including a lost response. On 09-27 the
  Italy phone left a job with no upload attempt, which fits a request that
  landed on the server while the response never came back.
- **A refused job creation no longer leaves the progress panel** covering
  the error message.

Tested in headless Chrome against the dev server with the network mocked.
A failed upload followed by a retry makes 1 job; the previous `DropZone`
makes 2 on the same test.

**Deployed with `vercel --prod` on 2026-09-29 after the owner said "ship it".
Checked live on scenefixer.com: an abandoned job shows the new message.**

## The bug: a failed scan stayed counted

`bhxeogif` (German user, `Englisch.Szene1.mp4`, 18.6 MB) failed in ffmpeg
normalise. The file has `ftyp`, `free`, then an open-ended `mdat` and **no `moov`**:
an unfinished recording, not damaged by our upload. The scan was counted before
analysis and only refunded on an Anthropic outage, so the user was refused five
times ("That's your free scan for today"). Their re-exports (19.2 MB, then 18.8 MB)
reached storage and the 18.8 MB one is valid (H.264 + AAC, 141 s). A second account
then uploaded the same size and got a scan.
